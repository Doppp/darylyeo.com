AI Transparency Statement

I use AI tools in parts of my writing workflow on this site.

Posts published here reflect my own ideas, judgment and conclusions. AI may help at the edges: tightening language, improving flow, catching awkward phrasing or helping me test whether an explanation can be made simpler and clearer.

I also use AI conversationally during development of an essay, much like I would use notes, outlines or a second pair of eyes. It can be useful for pressure-testing an argument, exploring alternative structures or surfacing angles I may want to pursue or reject.

What it does not do is replace authorship. The thinking, selection and final responsibility for what appears on this site are mine.
